# 10. Setting up a new member

[← Previous: All certificates](09-certificates.md) · [Table of Contents](README.md) · [Next: Members: manage your crew →](11-members.md)

**Who can use it:** managers who can see and edit their crew ([chapter 11](11-members.md)). Step 1 is done on **admin.ngft.com**.

## What it is for

A new person joins your company. Work through these steps one by one. Each step says where to find the details.

## Step by step

1. **Add the person.** On **admin.ngft.com**, add or invite the person and give them their **role**. You need the role **Company Administrator** for this. The person receives an e-mail that explains how to sign in.
2. **Open the new member.** In NGFT Crew, click **Members** in the sidebar. A new member shows a **warning triangle**, because the setup is not finished. Click the triangle, or the person icon under **Action** ([chapter 11](11-members.md)).
3. **Give the properties.** Choose the **role**, **position** and **operation** of the member and click **Save** ([chapter 12](12-member-edit.md)).
4. **Aircraft and operations.** Open the tab **Aircraft & Operation Authorization / Limitations** and assign the aircraft and the operations ([chapter 12](12-member-edit.md)).
5. **Upload existing certificates**, for example sign-offs ([chapter 8](08-upload-certificate.md)). A certificate you upload as a manager waits for approval ([chapter 18](18-approve-courses.md)). To upload several at once, and have them approved right away, use the bulk upload ([chapter 19](19-bulk-upload.md)).
6. **Buy the necessary courses**, or a bundle ([chapter 6](06-all-courses-and-bundles.md), [chapter 7](07-cart-and-payment.md) and [chapter 14](14-subscriptions.md)).

   > **Always start with the Initial.** EASA requires an initial course first, so a crew member only turns green once the initial is completed. A refresher does not count before that. If you have your original Initial certificate at hand, you can upload it if you want ([chapter 8](08-upload-certificate.md)). However, it is a new system, so why not start new?

7. **The member signs in.** With the e-mail from step 1 the member signs in, sees their courses and starts them ([chapter 1](01-getting-started.md) to [chapter 4](04-my-courses.md)).
8. **Check.** Later, open the **Position Overview** and see whether the member is green ([chapter 13](13-overviews.md)). If a course is missing that should be there, check the Relations Tree ([chapter 17](17-relations-tree.md)).
