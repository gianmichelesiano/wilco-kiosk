# Setting up a new member

**Who can use it:** managers who can see and edit their crew. Step 1 is done on **admin.ngft.com**.

## What it is for

A new person joins your company. Work through these steps one by one. Each step says where to find the details.

## Step by step

1. **Add the person.** On **admin.ngft.com**, add or invite the person and give them their **role**. You need the role **Company Administrator** for this. The person receives an e-mail that explains how to sign in.
2. **Open the new member.** In NGFT Crew, click **Members** in the sidebar. A new member shows a **warning triangle**, because the setup is not finished. Click the triangle, or the person icon under **Action**.
3. **Give the properties.** Choose the **role**, **position** and **operation** of the member and click **Save**.
4. **Aircraft and operations.** Open the tab **Aircraft & Operation Authorization / Limitations** and assign the aircraft and the operations.
5. **Upload existing certificates**, for example sign-offs. A certificate you upload as a manager waits for approval in **Approve Courses**. To upload several at once, and have them approved right away, use the **Bulk Certificate Upload**.
6. **Buy the necessary courses**, or a bundle.

   > **Always start with the Initial.** EASA requires an initial course first, so a crew member only turns green once the initial is completed. A refresher does not count before that. If you have your original Initial certificate at hand, you can upload it if you want. However, it is a new system, so why not start new?

7. **The member signs in.** With the e-mail from step 1 the member signs in, sees their courses in **My Courses** and starts them.
8. **Check.** Later, open the **Position Overview** and see whether the member is green. If a course is missing that should be there, check the Relations Tree.
