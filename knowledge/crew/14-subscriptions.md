# 14. Bundles and active subscriptions

[← Previous: Reports](13-overviews.md) · [Table of Contents](README.md) · [Next: Company custom certificates and LMS courses →](15-company-courses.md)

**Who can use it:** managers with the permission *Buy courses for crew members*.

By default: Accountable Manager (AM), Chief Flight Instructor (CFI), Company Account Owner, Company Administrator, Deputy NPCT, Head of Training (HT), NPCA, NPCT, NPFO, NPGO and NPMO.

UAS …: the same roles for drone operations.

![Where you are: Active Subscriptions](screenshots/13-00-where.png)

## What it is for

A **bundle** buys all the courses a role or position needs. It is usually bought by the NPCT for their users, and it works as a subscription:

- Every year the latest course is enrolled automatically.
- If you buy an **Initial** bundle, your crew member automatically gets the **refresher** the following year.
- The invoice is sent automatically.
- Nothing is forgotten, and the crew member always stays current.
- **Refresher bundles cost less** than buying the same courses one by one.

**Active Subscriptions** (menu **Members Properties**) is only the overview: it lists who is subscribed to which bundle and when it renews. It is empty when nobody has a bundle yet. **You buy a bundle on the member's own page.**

## Step by step

### Buy a bundle for a member

> **Always start with the Initial.** EASA requires an initial course first, so a crew member only turns green once the initial is completed. A refresher does not count before that. If you have your original Initial certificate at hand, you can upload it if you want ([chapter 8](08-upload-certificate.md)). However, it is a new system, so why not start new?

1. Click **Members** in the sidebar and search for the member.
2. Click the briefcase icon in the member's row.

   ![Member row](screenshots/13-01-member-icons.png)

3. Open the tab **Bundles** (red frame) and click the cart icon (**1**) of the bundle you want. Then pay as described in [chapter 7](07-cart-and-payment.md).

   ![Bundles tab](screenshots/13-02-bundles-tab.png)

   A bundle that is already subscribed shows *Subscribed to the bundle* instead of the cart icon, so it cannot be bought twice.

### See the subscriptions

1. Click **Members Properties** in the sidebar, then **Active Subscriptions** (red frame above).
2. The table shows **Members**, **Bundle** and **Renewal Date**. Use **Search** to find a member or a bundle.

> **Good to know:** if a member is removed from the crew, the bundle is deleted automatically.
