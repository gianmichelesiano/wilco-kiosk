# 6. Other courses and bundles

[← Previous: DG and CRM trainings](05-my-trainings.md) · [Table of Contents](README.md) · [Next: Shopping cart and payment →](07-cart-and-payment.md)

**Who can use it:** all users (menu **My Courses**, tabs **All Courses** and **Bundles**). Managers see the same tabs on a member's page.

![Where you are: My Courses](screenshots/06-00-where.png)

## What it is for

Normally you buy a course in **My Courses**: click the cart icon in the row of the course ([chapter 4](04-my-courses.md)). This chapter covers two other cases.

**All Courses** lists every e-learning course available in NGFT Crew. Use it if you have not been assigned a new role yet, or if you want to take a course that is not in your profile. You can still find it here.

**Bundles** buy and unlock all the courses a role or position needs with one click. Refresher bundles cost less than the single courses.

> **Always start with the Initial.** EASA requires an initial course first, so a crew member only turns green once the initial is completed. A refresher does not count before that. If you have your original Initial certificate at hand, you can upload it if you want ([chapter 8](08-upload-certificate.md)). However, it is a new system, so why not start new?

The icons are the same as in [chapter 4](04-my-courses.md).

**Managers:** on a member's page (**Members**, briefcase icon) you see the same tabs, including **All Courses** and **Bundles**. With the permission *Buy courses for crew members* you can buy for the member (bundles: [chapter 14](14-subscriptions.md)).

## Step by step

1. Click **My Courses** in the sidebar and open the tab **All Courses** or **Bundles** (red frames).

   ![All Courses](screenshots/05-01-all-courses.png)

2. Type in **Search** to find a course.

3. Click ![Play](screenshots/icons/action-play.svg) to start a free course, or ![Cart](screenshots/icons/action-cart.svg) to add a course or bundle to your cart ([chapter 7](07-cart-and-payment.md)).
