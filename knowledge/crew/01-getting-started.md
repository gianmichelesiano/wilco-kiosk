# 1. Signing in and the main screen

[← Table of Contents](README.md) · [Next: Selecting your company →](02-company-selector.md)

**Who can use it:** all users.

## What it is for

NGFT Crew shows which courses and certificates you need, which are valid and which are missing.

Open it at **https://co.ngft.com**, or start from the NGFT Cockpit at **https://my.ngft.com** and open NGFT Crew there. The same login works for both.

## Step by step

### Sign in

1. Open https://co.ngft.com.
2. Enter your **Email** (**1**) and **Password** (**2**).
3. Click **Sign In** (**3**).

   ![Sign in](screenshots/01-01-sign-in.png)

### Set a new password

- **You cannot sign in:** click **Forgot Password?** (**4**) and follow the instructions in the e-mail you receive.
- **You are signed in:** open **https://my.ngft.com**, click your initials at the top right and open **My Profile**. You can change your password there.

### The main screen

After signing in you see **My Courses**. The screen has three areas: the **top bar**, the **menu on the left** and the **page** in the middle.

![Main screen](screenshots/01-02-main-screen.png)

The top bar:

![Top bar](screenshots/01-03-header.png)

1. **Logo**: back to the start page.
2. **Company**: the company you work in ([chapter 2](02-company-selector.md)).
3. **Your name**.
4. **Gear icon**: your profile ([chapter 3](03-user-profile.md)).
5. **Cart**: your shopping cart ([chapter 7](07-cart-and-payment.md)).
6. **Logout**: sign out.
7. **Grid icon**: back to the Cockpit.

### Help inside NGFT Crew

- **? button** in the top bar: opens this tutorial.
- **Wilco** is your help assistant. Ask him how to do something in NGFT Crew, for example how to buy a course or upload a certificate.

**Wilco only knows what is written in this tutorial.** He reads his answers from it. If something is missing from the tutorial, Wilco cannot find it either. If you do not get an answer, write to **support@ngft.com**.

### What you see depends on your role

Your **role** (for example *Pilot*) decides which menu items you have. Pilots see **My Courses** and **My Trainings**. Managers, such as the NPCT, see more, for example **Members**. If an item described here is missing, your role does not include it. Ask your NPCT.

### Sign out

Click the **logout** icon (**6**) in the top bar.

## If you see a message instead of a page

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **No active company** | Select another company ([chapter 2](02-company-selector.md)) |
| **Payment required** | Ask your company administrator to renew the subscription |
| **Permission denied** | You have no access to this page. Ask your administrator |
| **Server error** | Reload the page. If it stays, contact NGFT support |
