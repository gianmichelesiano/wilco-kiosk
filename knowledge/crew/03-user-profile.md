# 3. Your profile

[← Previous: Selecting your company](02-company-selector.md) · [Table of Contents](README.md) · [Next: My Courses →](04-my-courses.md)

**Who can use it:** all users.

## What it is for

Your profile holds your personal data, such as your name, e-mail address and phone number.

> **To change your profile** (e-mail address, phone number and so on), go to **https://my.ngft.com** and click your **initials at the top right**. You cannot change it inside NGFT Crew.

## Step by step

1. Click the gear icon (**1**) at the top.
2. Click **Profile** (**2**).
