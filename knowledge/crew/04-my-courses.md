# 4. My Courses: see what you need and start or purchase your course

[← Previous: Your profile](03-user-profile.md) · [Table of Contents](README.md) · [Next: DG and CRM trainings →](05-my-trainings.md)

**Who can use it:** all users (menu **My Courses**). This is the first page after sign-in.

![Where you are: My Courses](screenshots/04-00-where.png)

## What it is for

**My Courses** shows the courses you need and whether they are valid. They depend on your **roles** (for example *Pilot*), **positions** (for example *CAT Pilot*) and **operations**, which your manager sets up for you.

The tabs **Positions**, **Operations** and **Aircraft** show the same courses grouped differently. **All Courses** and **Bundles** are in [chapter 6](06-all-courses-and-bundles.md).

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ![Green](screenshots/icons/status-valid.svg) | **Valid** |
| ![Orange](screenshots/icons/status-buffer.svg) | **Buffer zone**: the certificate expires soon, usually within 3 months. You and your NPCT get an e-mail when it enters the buffer zone, again 10 days before it expires and once more the day before. |
| ![Red](screenshots/icons/status-expired.svg) ![Red](screenshots/icons/status-missing.svg) | **Expired or missing** |

| Icon | Action |
|---|---|
| ![Play](screenshots/icons/action-play.svg) | Start the course |
| ![Cart](screenshots/icons/action-cart.svg) | Buy the course ([chapter 7](07-cart-and-payment.md)) |
| ![Upload](screenshots/icons/action-upload.svg) | Upload an external certificate ([chapter 8](08-upload-certificate.md)) |
| ![Download](screenshots/icons/action-download.svg) | Download your certificate (optional) |
| ![History](screenshots/icons/action-history.svg) | Show history |

## Step by step

### See what you need

1. Click **My Courses** in the sidebar (red frame).

2. Click a position (**2**), or **Expand All Positions** (**1**) at the far right next to the role.

   ![Positions](screenshots/04-01-positions.png)

3. Read **Valid until** for each course.

### Buy a course you are missing

1. In **My Courses**, find the course you are missing. If it has an arrow, use the **first row**.
2. Click the cart icon ![Cart](screenshots/icons/action-cart.svg) at the end of the row. Then pay as described in [chapter 7](07-cart-and-payment.md).

### Start your course

1. Click the **play** icon (**1**). The arrow (**2**) shows the refresher courses. A refresher only counts after you have completed the initial.

   ![Courses of a position](screenshots/04-02-position-courses.png)

   **You do not have to open the arrow.** The system knows which course is the next one for you. The **first row** is the one that counts: its status and date are the current ones, and you buy, start or upload right there with the icons at the end of that row.

   ![First row of a course](screenshots/04-06-first-row.png)

2. Moodle opens in a new window. If not, allow pop-ups. Courses usually have four parts:

   ![Moodle course](screenshots/04-04-moodle-sections.png)

   1. **General**: read the instructions.
   2. **Course Material**: open the module, choose your language, click through the pages and finish with **Exit course**.
   3. **Final Tests**: complete the test.
   4. **Feed-back**: give your feedback and click **Reply**.

3. Close the Moodle window. In NGFT Crew your status is now updated and your certificate is saved safely in the system. You do not have to download it, but you can: click the arrow icon ![Download](screenshots/icons/action-download.svg).

   ![Valid course with certificate](screenshots/04-05-certificate.png)
