# Orders and invoices

**Who can use it:** managers with the permission *Order read* (menu **Orders Crew**).

By default: Accountable Manager (AM), Accountant, Backoffice / General Staff, Company Administrator and NPCT.

UAS …: the same roles for drone operations.

![Where you are: Orders Crew](screenshots/20-00-where.png)

## What it is for

**Orders** is the purchase history of your company. Every checkout from the shopping cart creates an order. Here you follow its payment status and download the invoice or receipt.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Paid** | Payment received, courses are available |
| **Processing** | Payment is not yet confirmed |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled. Only NGFT can cancel an unpaid order |

The **payment type** is shown as *Invoice*, *Stripe* (credit card) or *PayPal*.

## Step by step

1. Click **Orders Crew** in the sidebar (red frame above). The page is titled **Orders**.
2. Find the order in the list. Check the date, the buyer, the courses, the **status** and the **payment type**.
3. In the column **Invoice**, click ![Download](screenshots/icons/order-download.svg) to download the invoice, or ![QR code](screenshots/icons/order-qr.svg) to download the QR code for the payment. If the document is not available you see *Document not found*.
