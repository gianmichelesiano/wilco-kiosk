# 22. Trainer, Instructor, Assessor and Checker

[← Previous: Orders and invoices](21-orders.md) · [Table of Contents](README.md) · [Next: Troubleshooting →](23-troubleshooting.md)

**Who can use it:** people who have one of these roles in the company. The permissions come with the role.

![Where you are: DG/CRM Crew Training](screenshots/21-00-where.png)

## What it is for

DG and CRM trainings have several parts ([chapter 5](05-my-trainings.md)). Each part is done with a person who has a specific role. These people have an extra menu item under **DG/CRM Crew Training**, with the trainings assigned to them.

| Menu item | Role in the system | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Trainer (Classroom)** (**1**) | Theoretical Knowledge Instructor | NGFT sign-off of the classroom part |
| **Instructor (Practical)** (**2**) | CBTA Instructor | The practical instruction |
| **Assessor** (**3**) | CBTA Assessor | The assessment |
| **Checker** (**4**) | Flight Checker | Coming soon: sign-off of OPCs etc. |

A person can have several of these roles and then sees several menu items.

## Step by step

1. In the sidebar, under **DG/CRM Crew Training**, click the item for your role.
2. The list shows the trainings assigned to you, with **Training**, **Status** and the parts **e-learning**, **Classroom** and **Practical**. Use **Search** to find one. The list is empty as long as no training is assigned to you.
