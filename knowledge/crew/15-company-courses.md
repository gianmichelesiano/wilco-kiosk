# Company custom certificates and LMS courses

**Who can use it:** managers with the permission *Configure company custom courses* (menu **Company Custom Courses**).

By default: Accountable Manager (AM), Chief Flight Instructor (CFI), Company Account Owner, Company Administrator, Company LMS Manager, Deputy NPCT, Head of Training (HT), NPCA, NPCT, NPFO, NPGO and NPMO.

LMS courses also need *Configure company LMS course*. By default: Company LMS Manager.

UAS …: the same roles for drone operations.

![Where you are: Company Custom Courses](screenshots/14-00-where.png)

## What it is for

Your company can add two different things:

- **Custom certificates** are your company's own certificates, for example an internal briefing. You only create the name in the system. Members upload the proof themselves. This is quick and simple.
- **Company LMS courses** are online courses that your company builds on the NGFT learning platform (Moodle). This needs the add-on **Courses** and the role **Company LMS Manager**.

## Custom certificates

You define your company's **own certificates**, for example an internal briefing. They appear in your members' course lists, and members can upload proof for them.

### Step by step

1. Click **Company Custom Courses** in the sidebar, then **Custom Certificate** to see the list.
2. To add one, click **Add Custom Certificate** (red frame above) and fill in the name (**1**), the default validity period (**2**) and, optionally, a theme and the positions it applies to.
3. Click **Save** (**3**). Use **Status** to switch a certificate on or off.

   ![Add Custom Certificate](screenshots/14-02-add-custom-certificate.png)

## Your own online courses (Company LMS)

Your company can also offer **its own online courses**. You build them yourself on the NGFT learning platform (Moodle), the same platform the NGFT courses run on. Your members take them like any other course.

The first course takes some effort. Once it is set up, you can reuse its structure for your other courses and also for the following years.

**What you need:**
- The add-on **Courses** for your Crew Overview subscription. You buy it on **admin.ngft.com** (there are packages Basic, Pro and Enterprise, with different numbers of courses, and a one-year minimum commitment). Courses are usually valid for about a year, so the add-on runs for the same period.
- With the add-on you get a protected area on the NGFT server for your own courses, and the role **Company LMS Manager**. People with this role create and manage the courses. In Crew they see the menu items **Company LMS Course** and **Add LMS Company Course**.

### Step by step

1. **Book the add-on.** On **admin.ngft.com**, add the add-on **Courses** to your Crew Overview subscription.
2. **Give the role.** On **admin.ngft.com**, give the person who will build the courses the role **Company LMS Manager**.
3. **Prepare the course in NGFT Crew.** The Company LMS Manager signs in, clicks **Company Custom Courses** in the sidebar and then **Add LMS Company Course** (red frame).

   ![Add LMS Company Course](screenshots/14-03-lms-menu.png)

   Fill in the form. Example: a course for your base staff.

   ![Form](screenshots/14-04-lms-form.png)

   1. **Course Full Name:** for example *Safety Procedures at the Base*.
   2. **Default validity period:** how long the certificate is valid after the course.
   3. **Buffer Zone:** how long before it expires the course turns orange. Usually 3 months.
   4. **Theme:** the group in which your course appears in the Crew Overview report, for example *Licenses* or *Sign-Offs*.
   5. **Authorized Signatory:** the person who signs the certificate.
   6. **Certificate Summary (PDF):** the regulation or procedure your course is based on, for example *our Safety Manual, chapter 4*. The certificate says: *We hereby confirm that the (your course) Course according to (your text) has been successfully completed by (name).* So do not write a full sentence.
   7. **Is Refresher:** leave it off if it is an **Initial** course. Switch it on only if this course is a **Refresher** of another course.
   8. Click **Create**.

   The short name, the display name and the other summary are optional. The list **Company LMS Course** shows your courses and how many of the courses in your add-on you have used.
4. **Find your course in Moodle.** NGFT Crew has created your course in Moodle for you, still empty, and you are its **Teacher**. Go to **https://elearning.ngft.com** and sign in with your NGFT account. Click **My courses** (**1**). Your course is there (**2**), with the name of your company below it.

   ![My courses in Moodle](screenshots/14-05-moodle-my-courses.png)

5. **Build the course.** Open it and switch on **Edit mode** (**1**, top right). In each section you add content with **Add an activity or resource** (**2**).

   ![Edit mode](screenshots/14-06-moodle-edit-mode.png)

   How you build the course is up to you. Only the content you want to teach is needed. If you like, you can use the structure of the NGFT courses, so your members find their way:
   1. **General:** a short welcome text. Say what the course is about and what to do.
   2. **Course Material:** one **Page** (**1**) for each topic. Our example: *Why safety at the base matters*, *Fire safety and evacuation*, *Fuelling and ramp safety* and *Reporting hazards and incidents*.

      ![Add an activity or resource](screenshots/14-07-moodle-chooser.png)

   3. **Final Test:** a **Quiz** to check what your members have learned, for example *Safety Procedures - Final Test*. How to fill it with questions is explained below.
   4. **Feedback:** a **Feedback** form, for example *Your feedback*, if you want to hear what your members think.
   5. **Course Completed:** a **Text and media area** with a short congratulation, for example *Congratulations! You have completed the course successfully.*

   **Add questions to your quiz.** The quiz is empty at first. We add one multiple choice question:
   1. Open the quiz and click **Questions**. Click **Add** and then **a new question**.
   2. Choose **Multiple choice** (**1**) and click **Add** (**2**).

      ![Choose the question type](screenshots/14-10-quiz-type.png)

   3. Give the question a **name** (**1**), only you see it, and write the **question text** (**2**), for example *Where do you go when the fire alarm sounds?*

      ![Name and text](screenshots/14-11-quiz-question.png)

   4. Scroll down to the answers. Write one possible answer for each choice (**1**). Set the **grade** (**2**) of the right answer to **100%** and leave the wrong ones on **None**.

      ![Answers](screenshots/14-12-quiz-answers.png)

   5. Scroll to the bottom and click **Save changes**. The question is now in your quiz. Repeat for every question.

      ![The quiz with one question](screenshots/14-13-quiz-done.png)

   Pages, quizzes and feedback forms are standard Moodle activities. Moodle explains them in its own help.

6. **Use your course again** (for example for next year). You do not have to build everything again.
   1. First create the new course in NGFT Crew, with **Add LMS Company Course** as in step 3. For the following year this is usually a **Refresher**: switch on **Is Refresher**.
   2. In Moodle, open the new course. Click **More** and then **Course reuse** (**1**).

      ![Course reuse](screenshots/14-14-moodle-reuse-menu.png)

   3. Click **Import** (**1**).

      ![Import](screenshots/14-15-moodle-reuse-import.png)

   4. Choose your old course (**1**) and click **Continue** (**2**). On the next pages click **Next** and at the end **Perform import**. The standard settings are fine.

      ![Choose the old course](screenshots/14-16-moodle-import-select.png)

   Your new course now has the same sections, pages, quiz with its questions and feedback form. No user data is copied. Change what is different this year, for example the texts and the year.

7. **Sort your courses into folders** (optional). With several courses it helps to have folders, for example *Pilot courses* and *Ground staff courses*. On **My courses**, click **Manage courses**. Click your company's folder, then **Create new category** (**1**) and give the new folder a name. To move a course, open the course, click **Settings** and choose the folder at **Course category**.

   ![Folders in Moodle](screenshots/14-08-moodle-folders.png)

> **Good to know:**
>
> - **Don't forget the Relations Tree.** Add your new course to your Relations Tree, so that it shows up for your members.
> - **Your members always start the course in NGFT Crew** with the play arrow. They do not need to sign in to Moodle themselves. Completing the course and getting the certificate also go through NGFT Crew.
> - **You are responsible for your content.** NGFT gives you an introduction. The creation of the course is then your responsibility. NGFT can also create a course for you on request. This is charged.
> - **Data protection:** NGFT complies with the applicable data protection laws, in particular the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection.
