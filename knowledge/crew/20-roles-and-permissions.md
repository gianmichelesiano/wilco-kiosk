# Roles and permissions

**Who can use it:** managers with the permission *Configure roles and permissions* (menu **Roles and Permissions**). People without this permission do not see the menu item.

By default: Accountable Manager (AM), Company Account Owner and Company Administrator.

UAS …: the same roles for drone operations.

![Where you are: Roles and Permissions](screenshots/19-00-where.png)

## What it is for

A **role** such as *Pilot* or *NPCT* is a bundle of **permissions**. The permissions decide which menu items and functions a person sees.

**Roles themselves cannot be changed here.** They are created and given to people in the NGFT admin. Which roles a person has, you see in **Members**. Here you only decide **which permissions a role has in your company**.

## Step by step

### See the permissions of a role

1. Click **Roles and Permissions** in the sidebar (red frame above).
2. The list shows all roles. Click a role to open its permissions.
3. Permissions in grey italics are NGFT standard and cannot be removed. A permission that your company added has a bin. Click the bin to remove it again.

### Give a role an additional permission

1. Choose the role (**1**). The permissions it has now appear in the field next to it.
2. Choose the permission you want to add (**2**).
3. Click **Add** (**3**).

   ![Role and permission](screenshots/19-01-roles.png)

If a role has been changed for your company, **Reset to defaults** appears next to **Add**. It takes the role back to the NGFT standard.

> **Careful:** permissions apply to **all** people with the role in your company. Check who has the role before you change it.
