# Uploading an external certificate

**Who can use it:** all users, for their own certificates.

## What it is for

Some entries are not courses but certificates that you have to upload yourself, for example your medical certificate. You can also use the upload button if you are new in the company and want to upload a valid certificate from before.

Upload your certificate here. It counts once a manager has approved it.

## Step by step

1. In your course list, click ![Upload](screenshots/icons/action-upload.svg) on the course.
2. Click **Choose File** (**1**) and select your certificate.
3. Enter the **Completion Date** (**2**). Add the **Valid Until** date, or tick **Certificate never expires**.
4. Click **Upload Certificate** (**3**).

   ![Upload form](screenshots/07-01-upload-form.png)

The course shows *Awaiting approval* until a manager has checked it.
