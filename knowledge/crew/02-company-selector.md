# Selecting your company

**Who can use it:** all users. You only need this if you work for more than one company.

## What it is for

Everything you see (courses, certificates, menu) belongs to the company shown at the top of the screen. If you work for several companies, switch here.

## Step by step

1. Click the three dots (**1**) next to the company name.
2. Click the company you want (**2**). The page reloads with that company.
