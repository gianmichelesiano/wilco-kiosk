# 19. Bulk certificate upload

[← Previous: Approving uploaded certificates](18-approve-courses.md) · [Table of Contents](README.md) · [Next: Roles and permissions →](20-roles-and-permissions.md)

**Who can use it:** managers with the permission *Bulk upload certificates* (menu **Bulk Certificate Upload**).

By default: Deputy NPCT and NPCT.

The Company Administrator does not have it by default.

UAS …: the same roles for drone operations.

![Where you are: Bulk Certificate Upload](screenshots/18-00-where.png)

## What it is for

Upload a certificate **for several members at once**, for example after an in-house training day, or several certificates **for one member**. You do the upload yourself, so the certificates are **approved right away**. They do not wait in **Approve Courses** ([chapter 18](18-approve-courses.md)).

| Menu entry | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **One Certificate, Multiple Users** | Several members completed the same course |
| **One User, Multiple Certificates** | One member has several certificates |

## Step by step

### One certificate, several members

1. Click **Bulk Certificate Upload** in the sidebar, then **One Certificate, Multiple Users** (red frame above). Choose the course or certificate and click **Next**.
2. **Users:** filter by role (**1**) if you like, choose the members (**2**) and click **Next** (**3**). **Add all users** selects everyone.

   ![Step Users](screenshots/18-02-users.png)

3. **Details:** the same file and the same dates apply to everyone you chose.

   ![Step Details](screenshots/18-03-details.png)

   - Choose the certificate file (**1**).
   - Enter the **Completion Date** (**2**) and the **Valid Until** date, or tick **Certificate never expires** (**3**). A **Comment** is optional.
4. Click **Upload & Approve** (**4**). The button stays grey until the required fields are filled in.

### One member, several certificates

1. Click **Bulk Certificate Upload** in the sidebar, then **One User, Multiple Certificates**.
2. **Person:** choose the member and click **Next**.
3. **Certificates:** choose the certificates and click **Next**. You can choose either NGFT courses or your custom certificates in one go, not both.
4. **Details:** as above. The file and the dates apply to all certificates you chose. Click **Upload & Approve**.
