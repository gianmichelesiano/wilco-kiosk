# Shopping cart and payment

**Who can use it:** all users. Managers with the permission *Buy courses for crew members* can also buy for other members.

## What it is for

The cart collects the courses you want to buy and lets you pay in one go. The cart icon in the header is grey when empty and shows a dot when it contains items.

> **Buying a refresher?** Only after the initial. EASA requires an initial first, and a refresher does not count before it is completed. If you have your original Initial certificate at hand, you can upload it if you want. However, it is a new system, so why not start new?

## Step by step

1. Click the cart icon (**1**) on the course or bundle you want.
2. Then click the **shopping cart** in the top bar (**2**). A dot on it means there is something in your cart. **Careful:** the arrow right next to it is **Logout**.

   ![Where to click](screenshots/06-01-cart-icons.png)

3. Check the items and the **Total**. The bin (**1**) removes an item.
4. Click **Add Billing Address** (**2**) and fill in the invoice details. Don't forget the address of your accounting office, meaning the person who pays the bill.
5. Pay (**3**) with **Credit Card**, **PayPal** or, if it is enabled for you, **Invoice**.

   ![Shopping cart](screenshots/06-02-cart.png)

6. After payment the course shows the play icon. Click it to start.
