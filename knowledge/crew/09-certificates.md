# 9. All certificates

[← Previous: Uploading an external certificate](08-upload-certificate.md) · [Table of Contents](README.md) · [Next: Setting up a new member →](10-new-member.md)

**Who can use it:** all users (menu **My Courses → All Certificates**).

## What it is for

Your personal certificate folder. Your certificates are saved safely here; you do not have to download them. The tab **All NGFT Courses** lists certificates of NGFT courses, **All Custom Certificates** those of your company's own courses, such as uploads. The page stays empty until you have a certificate.

## Step by step

1. Click **My Courses** (**1**) in the sidebar, then **All Certificates** (**2**).

   ![Where to find All Certificates](screenshots/08-01-all-certificates-menu.png)

2. Choose the tab.
3. Download a single certificate with its arrow icon ![Download](screenshots/icons/action-download.svg), or all of them with **Download All Certificates** (top right).
