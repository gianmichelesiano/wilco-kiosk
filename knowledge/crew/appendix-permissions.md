# Appendix: Permissions reference

[← Table of Contents](README.md)

Permissions are given to **roles** (see [chapter 20](20-roles-and-permissions.md)). If a menu item is missing, your role lacks the permission in the table. The default roles are the starting point: your company can change them in **Roles and Permissions** ([chapter 20](20-roles-and-permissions.md)).

| Permission | Default roles | What it unlocks | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| See training data of crew members | Accountable Manager (AM), Base Manager, Chief Flight Instructor (CFI), Chief Theoretical Knowledge Instructor (CTKI), CMM CAMO, CMM Maintenance (Part 145), Company Account Owner, Company Administrator, Compliance Monitoring Manager (CMM), Deputy NPCT, Head of Training (HT), NPCA, NPCT, NPFO, NPGO, NPMO (Part 145), Safety Manager (SM), UAS Accountable Manager, UAS CMM, UAS NPCT, UAS NPFO, UAS Safety Manager | **Members**, **Members Properties** (Position, Crew, Aircraft Overview) | [11](11-members.md), [12](12-member-edit.md), [13](13-overviews.md) |
| Buy courses for crew members | Accountable Manager (AM), Chief Flight Instructor (CFI), Company Account Owner, Company Administrator, Deputy NPCT, Head of Training (HT), NPCA, NPCT, NPFO, NPGO, NPMO (Part 145), UAS Accountable Manager, UAS NPCT, UAS NPFO | Buying for other members, **Active Subscriptions** | [7](07-cart-and-payment.md), [14](14-subscriptions.md) |
| Configure company custom courses | Accountable Manager (AM), Chief Flight Instructor (CFI), Company Account Owner, Company Administrator, Company LMS Manager, Deputy NPCT, Head of Training (HT), NPCA, NPCT, NPFO, NPGO, NPMO (Part 145), UAS Accountable Manager, UAS NPCT, UAS NPFO | **Company Custom Courses** (custom certificates) | [15](15-company-courses.md) |
| Configure company LMS course | Company LMS Manager | Company LMS courses | [15](15-company-courses.md) |
| Configure aircrafts | Accountable Manager (AM), Company Account Owner, Company Administrator, NPCA, UAS Accountable Manager | **Company Aircraft** | [16](16-aircraft-and-limitations.md) |
| Manage aircraft operations | Company Administrator, Deputy NPCT, NPCT, NPFO | **Limitations** | [16](16-aircraft-and-limitations.md) |
| Configure courses | Accountable Manager (AM), Chief Flight Instructor (CFI), Company Account Owner, Company Administrator, Company LMS Manager, Head of Training (HT), NPCA, NPCT, NPFO, NPGO, NPMO (Part 145), UAS Accountable Manager, UAS NPCT, UAS NPFO (not the Deputy NPCT) | **Relations Tree** | [17](17-relations-tree.md) |
| Approve extern certificates | Accountable Manager (AM), Chief Flight Instructor (CFI), Company Account Owner, Company Administrator, Deputy NPCT, Head of Training (HT), NPCT, NPFO, NPGO, NPMO (Part 145), UAS Accountable Manager, UAS NPCT, UAS NPFO | **Approve Courses** | [18](18-approve-courses.md) |
| Bulk upload certificates | Deputy NPCT, NPCT, UAS NPCT | **Bulk Certificate Upload** | [19](19-bulk-upload.md) |
| Configure roles and permissions | Accountable Manager (AM), Company Account Owner, Company Administrator, UAS Accountable Manager | **Roles and Permissions** | [20](20-roles-and-permissions.md) |
| Order read | Accountable Manager (AM), Accountant, Backoffice / General Staff, Company Administrator, NPCT, UAS Accountable Manager | **Orders** | [21](21-orders.md) |

Normal users need no permission for **My Courses**, **All Certificates** and **My Trainings**. The training roles *Trainer*, *Instructor*, *Assessor* and *Checker* work through the role itself, not through a permission (see [chapter 22](22-training-roles.md)).
