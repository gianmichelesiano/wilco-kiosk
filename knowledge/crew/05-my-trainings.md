# 5. DG and CRM trainings

[← Previous: My Courses](04-my-courses.md) · [Table of Contents](README.md) · [Next: Other courses and bundles →](06-all-courses-and-bundles.md)

**Who can use it:** all users.

## What it is for

According to EASA, **Dangerous Goods (DG)** and **CRM** trainings have several parts. You find them in your course list under your position, like any other course. The menu item **My Trainings** lists them too.

## Dangerous Goods (DG)

**Initial** training has 3 parts:

1. **E-learning module.** Start it with the arrow ![Play](screenshots/icons/action-play.svg).
2. **Instruction** by your internal DG instructor.
3. **Assessment** by your internal DG assessor.

A **refresher** only needs parts 1 and 3.

Click the arrow to see your progress:

![Progress of a DG training](screenshots/05-01-dg-progress.png)

- **1**: the e-learning module. A green check means it is completed.
- **2**: you need your instructor's sign-off.
- **3**: you need to pass the assessment with your assessor.

Green means a part is completed, red means it is still open. Evaluator and date are filled in when it is done.

The instructor or assessor signs you off once the training is completed. Once all parts are completed, you get your certificate.

## CRM

CRM has 2 parts:

1. **E-learning module.**
2. **Short classroom session (virtual)** with a certified CRM teacher. You find the dates on **https://my.ngft.com** under *Messages*, or ask your NPCT. At the moment you register for a session in a Doodle poll, not in NGFT Crew.

Click the arrow to see your progress:

![Progress of a CRM training](screenshots/05-02-crm-progress.png)

- **1**: start the e-learning module with the play icon.
- **2**: your attendance at the classroom session. Red until it is confirmed, then green.
