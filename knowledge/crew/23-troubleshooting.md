# 23. Troubleshooting: what do you need?

[← Previous: Trainer, Instructor, Assessor and Checker](22-training-roles.md) · [Table of Contents](README.md) · [Appendix: Permissions reference →](appendix-permissions.md)

**Who can use it:** everyone.

## What it is for

Something does not work as expected? Find your question and follow the link.

## For everyone

**I want to set a new password.**

- **You cannot sign in:** click **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page and follow the instructions in the e-mail ([chapter 1](01-getting-started.md)).
- **You are signed in:** open https://my.ngft.com, click your initials at the top right and open **My Profile**. You can change your password there.

**My course is not completed.**
- Did you finish **all parts** of the course in Moodle? Courses usually end with a feedback form. Open the course again with the play arrow and check ([chapter 4](04-my-courses.md)).
- Is it a **training** (DG or CRM)? Maybe one part, for example the e-learning, is done and the rest is still missing. Click the arrow to see your progress ([chapter 5](05-my-trainings.md)).

**A menu item is missing.**
Your role decides what you see. Ask your NPCT.

**I cannot find the answer in the tutorial, and Wilco does not know it either.**
Wilco answers only from this tutorial, so a topic that is missing here is missing for him too. Write to support@ngft.com ([chapter 1](01-getting-started.md)).

**A course I need is not in My Courses.**
Your manager gives you your roles, positions and operations ([chapter 12](12-member-edit.md)). If a course is still missing, ask your manager to check it. It might be missing in your company's Relations Tree ([chapter 17](17-relations-tree.md)).

**I uploaded a certificate, but it does not count yet.**
A certificate uploaded by a manager waits for approval ([chapter 18](18-approve-courses.md)).

## For managers

**The Overview still shows red, although the member has done the course.**
Click the refresh arrow to calculate again ([chapter 13](13-overviews.md)).

**An operation is missing at a member, for example Heliski, and I do not see it at the aircraft.**
- Check if the member has the operation assigned: **Members**, the person icon, tab **Properties**. If it is missing, choose role, position and operation and click **Save**. Only operations that belong to the chosen position are offered.
- Check if the aircraft is assigned: open the tab **Aircraft & Operation Authorization / Limitations**. If the aircraft is not in the list, choose it and click **Assign**.
- Open the aircraft. The operation is listed there. Switch **Allowed** on ([chapter 12](12-member-edit.md)).

**I want to track a personal document of my crew, for example an ID, a personal data sheet or a psychological assessment.**
See [Appendix: Good to know](appendix-good-to-know.md).

**I want to track ESET or First Aid training for my TSOPs.**
See [Appendix: Good to know](appendix-good-to-know.md).

**The tab Aircraft & Operation Authorization / Limitations or the menu item Limitations is missing.**
Either you do not have the right role ([chapter 20](20-roles-and-permissions.md)), or the aircraft have not been set up on admin.ngft.com ([chapter 16](16-aircraft-and-limitations.md)).

**My members do not see our own LMS course.**
Add the course to your Relations Tree ([chapter 17](17-relations-tree.md) and [chapter 15](15-company-courses.md)).

**I want to change my own course, but I cannot find it in Moodle.**
- You edit your course in Moodle. Sign in at elearning.ngft.com and click **My courses**. You are the Teacher of the courses you created. Switch on **Edit mode** ([chapter 15](15-company-courses.md)).
- Your members never need to sign in to Moodle themselves. They always start the course in CO with the play arrow ([chapter 4](04-my-courses.md)).
