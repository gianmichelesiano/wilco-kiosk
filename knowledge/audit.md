# NGFT Audit · WILCO_PRODUCT_GUIDE.md

### 1.1 What is NGFT Audit

**Short answer (Wilco):** NGFT Audit manages compliance audits of an aircraft operator, from planning to the closure of findings. You choose the requirements to check, for example EASA or company manuals, you conduct the audit, you write the report and you manage the findings with analysis of the causes and corrective actions assisted by AI.

- **Product name:** NGFT Audit. The footer of the app shows «Audit» and the videos call it «NGFT Audit Tool».
- **Who it is for:** the Compliance Monitoring and Safety department of helicopter and aircraft operators, CAMO, maintenance and training organizations: Compliance Monitoring Manager, auditor, Safety Manager and Accountable Manager.
- **Work it supports:**
  1. plan audits and see regulatory coverage gaps;
  2. prepare questions starting from the law text, from customized questions or generated by AI;
  3. conduct the audit with opening meeting, evaluation of the questions and closing meeting;
  4. write and finalize the report;
  5. manage findings with Root Cause Analysis (RCA), Corrective Action Plan (CAP), approval and closure;
  6. open a Change Management project starting from a finding;
  7. follow the indicators in the Audit Cockpit.
- **Requirements Sources:** «Regulations» (RegVault: for example CH-SR, EASA, G+, HeliOffshore, IOGP, TW-CAA) and «DocGen» (company documents divided by operating group: AOC, CAMO, MO, SPO, ATO, NCC, UAS).

### 1.3 Roles and visible modules

**Short answer (Wilco):** The modules you see depend on your role in the organization. Auditor, Safety Manager, Accountable Manager and Compliance Monitoring Manager create and plan audits. The Library is for Accountable Manager and Compliance Monitoring Manager. Configuration is only for Compliance Monitoring Managers.

To enter the app, you need at least one of these roles: NGFT Administrator, NGFT Support, Auditor, Safety Manager (SM), Accountable Manager (AM), Compliance Monitoring Manager (CMM), Compliance Monitoring Manager CAMO (CMM CAMO), Compliance Monitoring Manager Maintenance (CMM Maintenance Part 145). Anyone who has none of them is sent to the `/access-denied` page.

| Module (tab) | Roles that see it | Note on the backend |
|---|---|---|
| My Modules (home) and Audit Cockpit widget | All the roles above. The Cockpit widget appears only for SM, Auditor, AM and the three CMMs. | – |
| Create Audit, Audit Planning | SM, Auditor, AM, CMM, CMM CAMO, CMM Maintenance | Same roles to create audits. |
| All Audits, Findings | Roles above, plus NGFT Administrator and NGFT Support | The audit list of the backend does not include the NGFT roles. |
| Change Management | Like All Audits | The backend only accepts AM, Auditor and the three CMMs. |
| Library | AM, the three CMMs, NGFT Administrator, NGFT Support | Audit templates can be changed only by CMM, AM or NGFT Administrator. |
| Configuration | Only the three CMMs | Saving is accepted only for CMM, AM and NGFT Administrator. |

**Roles within the single audit** (Sources):
- **Team Lead:** the lead auditor. It is whoever creates the audit from the Create Audit page. Approves RCA, CAP and finding reports and becomes the approver of Change Management projects.
- **Supporting Auditor(s):** supporting auditors. They are chosen among SM, Auditor, AM and the three CMMs.
- **Auditee:** the manager of the audited area. Responds to findings with analysis, corrective actions and reports. Chosen among: AI Manager, Safety Manager, NPFO, NPCT, NPGO, the three CMMs, Accountable Manager, NPCA, Project Manager, Head of Training, CTKI, CFI.
- **External auditee(s):** users with role «Subcontractor / Supplier», plus free names for unregistered people.

**Verification.** Sources; the test account sees all tabs.

### 1.4 Who sees what data

- **All Audits:** whoever has access sees all the audits of the selected organization.
- **Findings and Audit Cockpit:** CMMs see everything. Others see only the audits they participate in as Team Lead, auditor, auditee or external auditee.
- **Organization:** data and permissions depend on the organization chosen in the «Organization» home field.

**Verification.** Sources.

### 3.1 How do I create an audit starting from regulations or company documents? (Create Audit)

**Short answer (Wilco):** Open Create Audit. Choose Regulations for regulatory requirements or DocGen for company manuals. Tick the articles or chapters and press Add to Audit. Then press Create Audit and Proceed with creation. The audit opens at the Plan step and you are Team Lead.

**Context**
- Role: Safety Manager, Auditor, Accountable Manager or one of the three CMMs.
- Device: desktop. On tablet the page follows the responsive layout (not verified).
- Initial screen: «Create Audit» tab, or the «Create Audit» card › «Open» on the home page.
- Prerequisites: correct organization selected in the home. For DocGen, the organization must have loaded documents.

**Steps**
1. Open «Create Audit». The page says «Select regulations and articles to create an audit».
2. Choose the «Regulations» or «DocGen» tab. If you change tab, the selection will clear.
3. In «Regulations» choose regulation, then part, then group of articles, and tick the articles. Each article can show the coverage status: «Covered», «Expiring Soon» or «Not Covered».
4. In «DocGen» choose the operating group («All», «AOC», «CAMO», «MO», «SPO», «ATO», «NCC», «UAS»), then the document, then the chapters.
5. Press «Add to Audit». In the «Add Articles to Audit» window (or «Add Chapters to Audit») confirm with «Add to Audit».
6. The «Create Audit (N)» button appears at the top, where N is the number of elements. Press it.
7. In the «Selected Articles» or «Selected Chapters» panel, check the list. You can use «Remove» or «Clear All».
8. Press «Proceed with creation». While saving, the button says «Creating...».

**Result**
- The page «Audit XX-REG-001» opens (or «XX-DOC-…» for DocGen). The number is made of the first two letters of the organization, the source and a sequential number.
- The audit is in the «Plan» step with «New» status. Team Lead is you and the date is the current month.
- It appears in All Audits and in the Audit Planning timeline.
- Team Lead receives the email «AuditCreated», if the email is active.

**If a button or result is missing**
- You do not see the «Create Audit» tab: your role is not among those that create audits.
- You do not see «Create Audit (N)»: you have not yet confirmed any item with «Add to Audit». The button only appears later.
- DocGen shows «0 documents»: the organization has no DocGen documents.
- Possible error messages: «Failed to create audit. Please try again.» or «Company not found. Please refresh the page.».

**Verification.** Observed in the app: Page, Regulations and DocGen tabs, DocGen Regulations and Groups list, «Create from template» button. The creation was not performed.

### 3.2 How do I plan audits and see which requirements remain unchecked? (Audit Planning)

**Short answer (Wilco):** Open Audit Planning. Planning Timeline shows audits of the next few months for each regulation. Gap Analysis shows how much of each part has already been verified and how many gaps remain. From there select the uncovered items and press Plan Audit.

**Context**
- Role: Safety Manager, Auditor, Accountable Manager or CMM.
- Device: desktop. The timeline has a fixed column and is not optimized for small screens.
- Initial screen: «Audit Planning» tab, showing «Full regulatory coverage across the audit cycle».
- Prerequisites: at least one type and one objective active in Configuration.

**Steps: Schedule from timeline**
1. Open the «Planning Timeline» tab.
2. Choose «Timeline View» or view of the month, and the horizon «12 months», «24 months» or «36 months».
3. On the regulation's row press the «Plan audit for this regulation» icon.
4. In the «Plan Audit for …» window fill in Year, Month, Team Lead, «Types» and «Objectives». They're all mandatory.
5. Press «Create Audit».

**Steps: Planning from gaps**
1. Open the «Gap Analysis» tab («Regulatory Coverage Analysis»).
2. Filter with «Source:» («RegVault», «DocGen», «All») and sort with «Highest Coverage» or «Lowest Coverage».
3. Open a card: it shows the percentage and number of elements «total», «covered» and «gaps».
4. Select articles or chapters with «Add to Selection».
5. Press «Plan Audit with N articles» (or «chapters»). It opens the same planning window, already filled in.

**Result**
- Message «Audit created successfully». The audit appears on the timeline as a planned block.
- The audit is in the step «Plan». The window alerts «This will create an audit in PLAN status. You can later add specific articles and chapters in the audit form.»
- If you press a block, the detail opens. While the audit is in Plan you can change month and year with «Save». «Open Audit» opens the complete audit.

**How coverage is calculated:** the cycle is fixed at 24 months.
- «Covered»: verified in the last 21 months.
- «Expiring Soon»: 3 months or less.
- «Not Covered»: over 24 months or never verified.

**If a button or result is missing**
- «Topic Analysis» shows only «coming soon»: the function is not yet available.
- The date of a block does not change: the audit has already passed the Plan step.
- There is no recurring planning. Each audit is individually planned.

**Verification.** Observed in the app: tabs, views, horizons, regulation list, Gap Analysis card with total, covered and gaps, text «coming soon».

### 3.3 How do I create an audit from a template? (Create from template, Audit Templates)

**Short answer (Wilco):** From a closed audit, save a template with Create Template. Then in Create Audit press Create from template, choose the template and press Use Template. The new audit inherits team, types, objectives and requirements.

**Context**
- Role: to create the audit, Safety Manager, Auditor, Accountable Manager or CMM. The Library is open to AM, CMM and NGFT.
- Device: desktop.
- Initial screen: «All Audits» to save the template; «Create Audit» or «Library» › «Audit Templates» to use it.
- Prerequisites: an audit at the Close step, because the «Create Template» menu appears only on closed audits.

**Steps**
1. In «All Audits» open the menu ⋮ of a closed audit and choose «Create Template».
2. To use the template, open «Create Audit» › «Create from template».
3. In the «Select Template» window, choose the template and press «Use Template».
4. Alternatively open «Library» › «Audit Templates», press «+» on the template and confirm «Create audit from this template?».

**Result:** a new audit opens at the Plan step. Team Lead, types, objectives, auditors, auditees and external auditees are copied. Articles and chapters are derived from the template's questions.

**If a button or result is missing**
- «Create Template» does not appear: the audit is not closed.
- «Audit Templates» shows «No audit templates found»: no template has yet been saved.
- A CMM CAMO or CMM Maintenance may receive an error when saving or deleting a template, because the backend only accepts CMM, AM and NGFT Administrator.

**Verification.** Observed in the app: «Create from template» button, menu item «Create Template» present only on audits at the Close step, tab «Audit Templates» blank.

### 3.4 How do I set the audit team, auditee and questions before an audit? (Audit › Plan and Prepare)

**Short answer (Wilco):** In the Plan step choose month, Team Lead, types and objectives. In the Prepare step add supporting auditors and auditees, and for each article prepare the questions: from the legal text, custom or generated by the AI. With Next the audit moves to Perform and the questions are locked.

**Context**
- Role: Team Lead or another auditor member (SM, Auditor, AM, CMM).
- Device: desktop.
- Initial screen: «All Audits» › ⋮ › «Resume» on an audit in Plan or Prepare. At the top there is the step bar «Plan», «Prepare», «Perform», «Reports», «Findings», «Close».
- Prerequisites: audit with at least one article or chapter selected.

**Steps: Plan**
1. In «Audit Details» choose year, month, «Team Lead», «Types» and «Objectives». Types and objectives are saved immediately.
2. In «Selected Regulations» check the requirements. To change them use «Edit Selection».
3. Press «Next».

**Steps: Prepare**
1. Choose «Supporting Auditor(s)» and «Auditee». They are saved immediately.
2. Write «Detailed description of audit objectives». The text is saved after about 2 seconds.
3. In «Select Questions», for each article or chapter choose one or more options:
   - «Use Law»: the text of the article becomes the question;
   - «Custom Question»: complete «Question» (mandatory), «Source content» and «Expected evidence»;
   - «Add AI generated question» or «Generate Question»: the question carries the badge «AI Generated» and is accepted or rejected with «Approve» and «Reject».
4. Press «Next» and confirm in the «Move to Perform step?» window.

**Result**
- The audit passes to the «Perform» step and the questions are created.
- Added auditors receive email «AuditorAssignedToAudit». The auditee receives «AuditeeAssignedToAudit» at each change, if the email is active.

**If a button or result is missing**
- A person does not appear in «Auditee»: his role is not among those admitted as an auditee.
- «Next» does not proceed: the auditee is missing, or an article is without questions (neither «Use Law» nor others).
- Possible validation messages: «Year is required», «Month is required», «At least one audit type must be selected», «At least one audit objective must be selected», «Team lead is required», «No regulation selected. Please select a regulation from the Create Audit page to continue.».
- The «Move to Perform step?» window also lists the auditee among the locked fields, but the auditee remains editable until the end of the audit (Sources, backend rule «update the auditee at any step except closed»).
- Supporting auditors and description are locked only after the opening meeting is confirmed.

**Verification.** Observed in the app: Plan screens («Audit Details», «Selected Regulations», «Edit Selection») and Prepare («Supporting Auditor(s)», «Auditee», «Detailed description of audit objectives») on test audits. No modifications performed.

### 3.5 How do I conduct the audit: opening meeting, questions and closing meeting? (Audit › Perform)

**Short answer (Wilco):** The Perform step has three stages. At the opening meeting you confirm place, time and participants and tick the checklist. Then you evaluate every question and attach the evidence. At the closing meeting, you confirm the findings with the auditee. On confirmation the audit moves to the report.

**Context**
- Role: Team Lead or auditor of the audit.
- Device: desktop or tablet, with responsive layout. Use on tablets has not been verified.
- Initial screen: «All Audits» › ⋮ › «Resume» on a Perform step audit. The phases are «Opening meeting», «Questions» and «Closing meeting».
- Prerequisites: audit with questions created and auditee assigned.

**Steps: Opening meeting**
1. In «Time and location» fill in «Audit date», «Time» and «Location *», then press «Confirm».
2. «Participants»: «Lead auditor (team lead)», «Supporting auditor(s)», «Auditee(s)».
3. In «External auditee(s)» add external users or free names, then press «Confirm». The button becomes «Confirmed».
4. Tick the 12 points of the «Checklist» or use «Check all». Add any «Remarks».
5. Press «Confirm meeting completed» and confirm «Confirm opening meeting completed?».

**Steps: Questions**
1. Each card shows «Question X of N», «Regulation Reference», «Legal Reference Text» and «Question».
2. Choose the rating in «Evaluation» and complete the fields.
3. Attach the evidence with «Upload Evidence» › «Upload». If the evidence arrives later use «Mark as pending».
4. You can leave with «Save & exit» or download the questionnaire with «Download Questionnaire».
5. Press «Proceed to closing meeting» and confirm «Proceed to closing meeting?».

**Steps: Closing meeting**
1. Repeat sections as in the opening. The checklist has 8 points.
2. At point 6, «Findings List», confirm each finding one by one.
3. Point 8, «Close meeting.».
4. Confirm «Confirm closing meeting completed?».

**Result:** the audit passes to the step «Reports». The auditee receives the email «AuditPerformed», if the email is active.

**If a button or result is missing**
- «Proceed to closing meeting» is disabled: evidence is still pending, or the mandatory description of a finding is missing.
- «Confirm meeting completed» does not work: a section is not confirmed or a checklist point is not ticked.
- An external user does not appear: the role «Subcontractor / Supplier» is needed.
- Point 6 of the closing does not appear: there are unconfirmed findings.

**Verification.** Observed in the app: opening meeting with sections, checklists and buttons «Confirm», «Confirmed», «Check all», «Confirm meeting completed». No confirmation performed.

### 3.6 How do I record a finding during an audit? (Audit › Perform › Questions)

**Short answer (Wilco):** On the question card choose Level 1, Level 2 or Observation and write the Finding Description. The finding is saved by itself, is created in Open status and appears in the Findings list.

**Context**
- Role: Team Lead or auditor of the audit.
- Device: desktop or tablet.
- Initial screen: audit at step Perform › «Questions».
- Prerequisites: confirmed opening meeting.

**Steps**
1. Read «Legal Reference Text» and «Question».
2. In «Evaluation» choose one of these options:
   - «Documented and implemented (Conformity)»
   - «Level 1 (Safety critical)»
   - «Level 2 (Safety relevant)»
   - «Observation»
   - «N/A»
3. For Level 1, Level 2 and Observation fill in «Finding Description *», which is mandatory.
4. If you need, add «Auditor Feedback», choose a category in «Select finding» (for example «Procedure not updated») and attach the evidence.

**Result**
- The choice is saved immediately; the texts are saved after about 2 seconds.
- The finding is created with «Open» status and appears in the Findings list with «Level 1 - Safety Critical», «Level 2 - Safety Relevant» or «Observation» level.
- It is confirmed at the closing meeting.
- The dates «RCA/CAP due date» and «closing due date» are set later, in the report or in the detail of the finding.

**If a button or result is missing**
- Message «Finding Description is required for this finding level»: the description is missing.
- The finding does not appear in the Findings list: check that the selected organization is that of the audit and that you participate in the audit (this applies to those who are not CMM).

**Verification.** Observed in the app: Findings list shows finding with «Level 2 - Safety Relevant» level and status «Open».

### 3.7 How do I prepare and finalize the audit report? (Audit › Reports)

**Short answer (Wilco):** In the Reports step, fill in the header and the chapters. You can insert texts from the Library and the list of findings. Save the draft, download the PDF preview and at the end press Finalize Report. The report becomes read-only and the audit switches to finding management.

**Context**
- Role: CMM, Team Lead or auditor assigned to the audit.
- Device: desktop.
- Initial screen: audit at the «Reports» step.
- Prerequisites: closing meeting confirmed, with all findings confirmed.

**Steps**
1. Fill in the header: «Title», «Name of Organization», «Address», «Content Reference», «Audit number», «Date of audit», «Date of report», participants, «External auditors».
2. Add chapters with «Add chapter» and subchapters with «Add sub chapter». Insert standard texts with «Insert from library» and the findings table with «Insert findings».
3. Set the finding deadlines in the report findings section.
4. Save with «Save draft». For a preview use «Download Draft».
5. If you want, reuse the structure with «Use template» or save it with «Save as template».
6. Press «Finalize Report» and confirm with «Finalize».

**Result**
- The report becomes read-only and can be downloaded as PDF with «Download Report».
- The audit passes to the step «Findings».
- When the draft is created, the Team Lead and the auditee receive the email «AuditReportCreated», if the email is active.

**If a button or result is missing**
- «Next» is disabled in this step: to advance you use «Finalize Report».
- «Finalize Report» fails: a chapter is empty. Each chapter must have content.
- The report is not created: there are unconfirmed findings, or you are not CMM, Team Lead or auditor of the audit.
- A Safety Manager may be unable to load the report: reading is allowed only to Auditor, AM and CMM.

**Verification.** Observed in the app: header and report sections on an audit at step Reports. No save performed.

### 3.8 How do I find a finding and check deadlines? (Findings)

**Short answer (Wilco):** Open Findings. The Open, In Progress and Closed tabs group the findings by status. For each finding you see level, status and the deadlines for RCA, CAP and closing. A red icon indicates an overdue deadline, a yellow one a deadline within three days. To open a finding, use the menu with three dots and then View.

**Context**
- Role: any user of the app. The CMMs see all the findings; the others only those of the audits they participate in.
- Device: desktop (table) or tablet (cards).
- Initial screen: tab «Findings».

**Steps**
1. Choose the tab:
   - «Open»: just recorded;
   - «In Progress»: under analysis, in approval or with a project;
   - «Closed».
2. Read the columns: «REGULATION / DOCUMENT», «QUESTION», «SOURCE AUDIT», «ASSIGNEE», «LEVEL», «DESCRIPTION», «STATUS», «DATE CREATED», «RCA/CAP DUE DATE», «CLOSING DUE DATE», «FEEDBACK», «EVIDENCE NEEDED».
3. With «Columns» choose the visible columns. The choice is kept for each tab.
4. To open a finding use ⋮ › «View». The «Source» link opens the original audit.

**Result:** the «Finding» page opens with description, deadlines, status, question, level, violated regulation, CAP, RCA result and gap analysis.

**Possible statuses:** «Open», «Analyzed», «RCA & CAP Submitted», «RCA & CAP Accepted», «RCA & CAP Rejected», «Project Started», «Report Submitted», «Report Approved», «Report Rejected», «Project Results Accepted», «Closed».

**If a button or result is missing**
- The column «ASSIGNEE» shows the auditee: there is no separate person responsible for the finding.
- The deadlines are set by hand and do not generate reminders.
- The detail shows «Failed to fetch finding»: this can happen to migrated findings of an organization, because the related question is not found ( Error 404 «Question not found»). Also check that you have selected the right organization.

**Verification.** Observed in the app: tabs, columns, «Export», «Columns», ⋮ › «View», link «Source», tablet card at 1024 px.

### 3.9 How do I analyze the root cause of a finding? (Finding › Analyze and Perform RCA)

**Short answer (Wilco):** Open the finding and press Analyze. The AI compares the expected state with the real one and you save the gap analysis. Then press Perform RCA and choose the method: five whys, quick classification with AI, fishbone diagram or manual classification. The result is classified according to the EASA taxonomy of the causes and you can change it before saving it.

**Context**
- Role: auditee of the audit. The backend also allows Team Lead, auditor and CMM.
- Device: desktop.
- Initial screen: «Findings» › ⋮ › «View», or the link in the email.
- Prerequisites:
  - the audit is in step «Findings», so the report is finalized;
  - the finding is in «Open», «Analyzed» or «RCA & CAP Rejected»;
  - NGFT AI service can be reached.

**Steps**
1. Press «Analyze». The button shows «Analyzing...» during processing.
2. In the «Analyze finding» panel, check and, if needed, edit «Gap Analysis Results»: «Expected State», «Actual State», «Gap Description», «Severity Rationale».
3. Press «Save Analysis». The finding goes to «Analyzed».
4. Press «Perform RCA». In «Select method» the AI pre-selects the recommended method:
   - «5 whys»: the AI asks up to five «why» questions;
   - «Quick AI Classifier»: automatic classification, about 30–60 seconds;
   - «Fishbone diagram»: analysis of six categories, estimated 8–12 minutes;
   - «Manual Classification»: you fill in «AREA (Level 1) *», «TYPE (Level 2) *», «FACTORS (Level 3) *» and «PRIMARY FACTOR *».
5. Press «Perform analysis». At the end you can change the result with «Edit» and confirm with «Done».
6. Press «Save».

**Result**
- Message «RCA result saved successfully».
- On the page appears «RCA Analysis Result» with the method, the confidence in percentage and the levels «AREA (Level 1)», «TYPE (Level 2)» and «PRIMARY FACTOR (Level 3)».
- To repeat the analysis use «Select Another Method / Re-analyze». The last saved result counts.

**If a button or result is missing**
- No analysis button: the audit is not yet in the Findings step, or the finding status does not allow it.
- «Analyze» is not there: a gap analysis or an RCA already exist. Use «Edit» on «Gap Analysis Results» or «View RCA Results».
- Message «No finding detected»: according to the AI the answer of the audit is compliant. Reread the finding description.
- You can't open the page from the email link: your role may not give access to the app.

**Verification.** Observed in the app: on a finding in «RCA & CAP Submitted» are visible «RCA Analysis Result» (method «QUICK_AI», confidences for AREA, TYPE and PRIMARY FACTOR) and «Gap Analysis Results». The analysis was not carried out.

### 3.10 How do I create and send the corrective action plan? (Finding › Create CAP and Submit RCA & CAP)

**Short answer (Wilco):** After the RCA press Create CAP. The AI proposes immediate, short and long-term actions, with priority and times. You can edit them or write a manual plan. Save with Save CAP and send everything to the auditor with Submit RCA & CAP.

**Context**
- Role: auditee of the audit.
- Device: desktop.
- Initial screen: page «Finding».
- Prerequisites: a saved RCA result, audit at Findings step, finding in «Analyzed» or «RCA & CAP Rejected».

**Steps**
1. Press «Create CAP». The «Corrective Action Plan» panel shows «Generating Corrective Action Plan...».
2. Choose the type of plan:
   - «AI Generated CAP»: it shows «Estimated Effectiveness», «Overall Strategy», «Immediate Actions», «Short-term Actions», «Long-term Actions» and «Monitoring Plan». For each action you can change priorities (High, Medium, Low) and «Timeline (days)».
   - «Manual CAP»: Write free text actions.
3. Press «Save CAP».
4. Press «Submit RCA & CAP» and confirm with «Submit».

**Result**
- Message «RCA & CAP submitted successfully». The state becomes «RCA & CAP Submitted».
- The Team Lead and all CMMs of the organization receive the email «RcaCapSubmitted», if the email is active.
- The auditor sees the approval buttons in the audit.
- To replace the plan use «Generate new CAP» and then «Replace CAP».

**If a button or result is missing**
- «Create CAP» is not there: a saved RCA is missing.
- «Submit RCA & CAP» is not there: RCA or CAP are not saved, or the finding has already been sent.
- Possible validation messages: «Please enter corrective action details», «No actions to save», «Please select a priority».

**Verification.** Observed in the app: CAP saved visible on the page («Corrective Action Plan», «1 action», «Action 1», priority «MEDIUM»). Generation and sending not performed.

### 3.11 How do I approve or reject RCA & CAP as an auditor? (Audit › Findings)

**Short answer (Wilco):** Open the audit at the Findings step. On a finding in RCA & CAP Submitted, select Approve RCA & CAP or Reject and confirm. After approval, the auditee can continue with a finding report or a change project. After rejection, the auditee can revise and resubmit.

**Context**
- Role: Team Lead, auditor or CMM.
- Device: desktop.
- Initial screen: «All Audits» › ⋮ › «Resume» on the audit at the Findings step, or the «Source» link in the Findings list.
- Prerequisites: finding in «RCA & CAP Submitted».

**Steps**
1. In the «Findings» step («Review the findings discovered during the audit. Comments can be added to each finding.») choose the level tab, for example «Level 2 - Safety Relevant (1)». Only levels that have findings appear.
2. Read «Corrective Action Plan», «RCA Analysis Result» and «Gap Analysis Results».
3. Press «Approve RCA & CAP» and confirm with «Approve», or «Reject» and confirm with «Reject».
4. To explain a rejection, write a comment with «Comments»: the rejection has no reason field.

**Result**
- The state becomes «RCA & CAP Accepted» or «RCA & CAP Rejected».
- The main auditee and all CMMs receive the email «RcaCapAccepted» or «RcaCapRejected», if the email is active.
- After approval the auditee sees «Create Report» and «Create Project».

**If a button or result is missing**
- The buttons do not appear on the finding's own page: approval is done only from the audit, at the Findings step.
- No button: finding is not in «RCA & CAP Submitted».

**Verification.** Observed in the app: on an audit at Findings step are visible «Comments», «Reject» and «Approve RCA & CAP» for a finding in «RCA & CAP Submitted». No pressed button.

### 3.12 How do I discuss a finding with the auditor or auditee? (Finding › Comments)

**Short answer (Wilco):** Each finding has the Comments button. A conversation opens where auditors, auditees and CMMs write messages with an attachment. You can edit or delete only your own messages.

**Context**
- Role: CMM, Team Lead, auditor, auditee or external auditee of the audit.
- Device: desktop or tablet.
- Initial screen: page «Finding» or step «Findings» of the audit.

### 3.13 How do I demonstrate the implementation of the actions and close a finding? (Finding Report and Close Finding)

**Short answer (Wilco):** When RCA and CAP are approved, the auditee presses Create Report, describes the actions carried out, attaches the evidence and sends it with Submit Report. The auditor approves the report and closes the finding with Close Finding.

**Context**
- Role: Auditee writes the report; Team Lead, auditor or CMM approve and close.
- Device: desktop.
- Initial screen: page «Finding» for the auditee; step «Findings» for the auditor.
- Prerequisites: finding in «RCA & CAP Accepted», without a related project.

**Steps**
1. Auditee: press «Create Report». In the «Create Report» panel, write the text and attach up to 5 files (PDF, JPEG, PNG, DOCX or XLSX, up to 100 MB each).
2. Auditee: press «Save», then «Submit Report». The state becomes «Report Submitted».
3. Auditor: in the «Findings» step of the audit press «Approve Report» or «Reject Report».
4. If the report is rejected, the auditee corrects it with «Edit Report» and resubmits it with «Resubmit Report».
5. Auditor: with the report approved, press «Close Finding» and confirm.

**Result**
- Message «Finding closed successfully». The state becomes «Closed» and the finding does not change anymore.
- The auditees and all CMMs receive the email «FindingClosed», if the email is active.
- When all findings are closed, the audit can switch to closing.

**If a button or result is missing**
- «Submit Report» is disabled: there are unsaved changes, or both text and attachments are missing.
- Message «Maximum number of attachments exceeded»: you have exceeded the 5 attachments.
- «Resubmit Report» may fail for an auditee who is not also auditor or CMM: the backend does not allow the transition from «Report Rejected» to «Report Submitted» for the auditee.

**Verification.** Sources.

### 3.14 How do I turn a finding into a Change Management project? (Change Management)

**Short answer (Wilco):** If a finding requires an organizational change, after RCA and CAP are approved press Create Project in the finding. The project has guided sections, with proposals from the AI. The audit's Team Lead approves it and the tasks are tracked on a board. When the project is closed, the finding becomes Project Results Accepted and the auditor can close it.

**Context**
- Role: Accountable Manager, Auditor or one of the three CMMs.
- Device: desktop. On tablet the list shows cards and the messages are in a dedicated tab.
- Initial screen: page «Finding». The «Change Management» list does not have a creation button.
- Prerequisites: finding in «RCA & CAP Accepted», without finding reports, audit at Findings step.

**Steps**
1. In the finding press «Create Project».
2. In the «Create Project» window fill in «Project title *», choose «Project type *» («Operational», «Procedural», «Emergency & Contingency», «Organizational», «Technological», «Compliance & Regulatory») and, if you want, a description.
3. The project in state «Draft» opens. Fill out the sections: «Initiation», «Impact Analysis», «Implementation Options», «Expected Timeline», «Budgeting», «Performance Metrics», «Recommendation». The empty sections are pre-filled by the AI. The text can be rewritten with «Make it shorter», «Rewrite it with more details» or «Simplify this text».
4. Press «Submit» and confirm «Submit Proposal». The state becomes «Proposed» and the project no longer changes.
5. The audit's Team Lead, automatically assigned as approver, presses «Approve» or «Reject».
6. With the project «Approved», go to «Board». Create tasks in the «Draft» column (Title and Deadline mandatory) and move them to «Evaluation».
7. When all tasks are in «Evaluation», the owner presses «Close Project».

**Result**
- In the finding the button becomes «View Project» and the state passes to «Project Started». At the end of the project it becomes «Project Results Accepted» and the auditor can use «Close Finding».
- «Project Overview» downloads the project as PDF with «Download PDF».
- Email (if sending is active):
  - to the author: when the project is created and submitted;
  - to the owner: on approval or rejection;
  - participants: when added.

**If a button or result is missing**
- The list does not have «Create Project»: the project is created only from a finding.
- «Create Project» is missing: the finding is not in «RCA & CAP Accepted», there is already a finding report, or audit is not in Findings step.
- «Close Project» is disabled: «To close the project all the tasks should be in evaluation.».
- «Approve» and «Reject» appear only to the approver. Anyone with another role receives an error.
- A Safety Manager sees the page but the backend may refuse data.
- A rejected project returns to draft with «Move to Drafts».

**Verification.** Observed in the app: list «Change Management» with filters and columns «Name», «Project Type», «Status», «Owner», empty state «No projects found», no creation button.

### 3.16 How do I read the status of audits and findings? (Audit Cockpit)

### 3.22 Why don't I see a module, a button or some data? (Quick diagnosis)

**Short answer (Wilco):** It usually depends on three things: your role, the selected organization and the step in which the audit is located. Many finding buttons only appear when the audit is at Findings step.

**Checks in order**
1. **Role:** the tab is missing from the menu if the role does not allow it. Page «Access denied»: no access role to the app. Page 403: role without access to that page.
2. **Organization:** check the «Organization» field in the home.
3. **Participation:** whoever is not CMM sees in Findings and Cockpit only the audits they participate in.
4. **Audit Step:**
   - Analyze, RCA, CAP, finding reports and project: only with audits at step «Findings».
   - Date change in Audit Planning: only at step «Plan».
   - «Create Template»: only on closed audits.
5. **Status of finding:** each button requires a precise status. A finding «Closed» no longer changes.
6. **Features not available:** Topic Analysis, member management, Settings, recurring planning, automatic reminders.

**Verification.** Sources; the modules not available were observed in the app.
